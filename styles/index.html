About Me

My name is Inga Locmele, and I’m a self-employed frontend web developer based on the Gold Coast, Australia, with 6+ years of professional experience.

I take on independent website projects and also work remotely with Delta4 Digital Inc., a digital marketing company based in Ontario, Canada. I joined Delta4 while living in Canada and have remained part of the team since relocating to Australia.

Through Delta4, I’ve contributed to hundreds of websites for businesses across Canada and the United States, ranging from template-based builds to fully customised sites with more complex frontend requirements.

I focus on creating responsive, accessible and user-friendly websites, with clean code and a consistent design.

Before moving into web development, I spent more than 10 years in database administration and information management, including leading a team of 10 employees.

My background in photography has strengthened my eye for composition, balance and visual detail. This shapes my approach to web design and development.
